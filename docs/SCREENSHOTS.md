Create `docs/screenshots` and save screenshots from your running application.
Use the final interface, readable zoom and demonstration records. Crop to relevant
content without hiding an error. No passwords, `.env` contents or real financial slips.

Upload screenshots 01–06 and 08 for report preparation. Screenshot 07 strengthens
responsive-design evidence. A final screenshot should show the actual application,
not a reference design or a generated mockup.

After adding files, link them in the README or report. Combine related screenshots
within the report rather than allocating one full page to every image.
